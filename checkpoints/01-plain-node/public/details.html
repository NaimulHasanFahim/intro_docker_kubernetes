Make it report itself as sick Its health check starts failing. Kubernetes stops sending it traffic, then restarts it and it comes back fine. Plain Docker does nothing — nobody is checking.
Crash it The app stops instantly, like a real crash. Kubernetes starts a replacement in seconds. A lone container just stays dead until you notice.
